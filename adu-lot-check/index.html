Pay for your ADU without giving up your low rate

If you locked in a low mortgage rate, you probably don't want to lose it. An ADU HELOC is a second loan on your home's equity, so your first mortgage stays exactly as it is.

Simple version: your current mortgage stays put. The HELOC pays for the ADU. Once the ADU is rented, that rent can help cover the payment.

Questions

Straight answers to common questions

Updated November 4, 2026

California ADU rules at a glance

  • Detached ADU: up to 1,200 sq ft on a single-family lot.
  • Attached ADU: up to half the size of the main home, capped at 1,200 sq ft.
  • Junior ADU: up to 500 sq ft, inside the existing home.
  • Impact fees: ADUs under 750 sq ft are exempt from city impact fees.
  • Garage conversions: cities generally can't require replacement parking.
  • Financing: an ADU HELOC is a second loan, so your first mortgage and rate stay in place.

Local rules vary. We confirm what applies to your address during the free lot check.

What is an ADU, exactly?

An accessory dwelling unit is a second, self-contained home on the same lot as your house. It has its own entrance, kitchen and bathroom. It can be a backyard cottage, a converted garage, an addition to your house, or a junior ADU inside it.

How much does an ADU cost in Southern California?

It depends mostly on the type and size. A garage conversion or junior ADU usually costs less than a new detached ADU, because part of the building already exists. Use the free lot check at the top of this page for a planning estimate. We give exact pricing after a free site visit.

Will I lose my low mortgage rate?

Not with a HELOC. A HELOC is a second loan, so your first mortgage and its rate stay in place. A cash-out refinance is different: it replaces your current mortgage with a new one.

Why does 749 square feet come up so often?

Under California law, ADUs under 750 sq ft are exempt from city impact fees. A well-designed 749 sq ft unit can fit two bedrooms and avoid those fees. We confirm the details with your city.

If I convert my garage, do I need to replace the parking?

In California, cities generally can't require you to replace parking when you convert a garage into an ADU. We check your city's rules during the free lot check.

Can the future ADU rent help me qualify for the loan?

Sometimes, depending on the loan program. Our loan officer will tell you which programs can count projected rent for your situation.

Do I need an appraisal for the HELOC?

Our ADU & Renovation HELOC uses an automated home valuation, so there's no in-person appraisal in most cases.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. We draw the plans, handle the structural and energy engineering, submit everything to the city and make any corrections the city asks for.

Who actually builds it?

MARS Construction Industries Corp, CSLB #1138550, with its own crews. MARS is licensed A, B, C-36 and C-20, and is bonded and insured.

Can I rent out my ADU?

Yes. California lets homeowners rent an ADU long term. Cities can require rentals to be longer than 30 days, so short-term vacation rentals are often not allowed.

Do I have to live on the property to build an ADU?

Not for a regular ADU. California no longer requires the owner to live on the property. A junior ADU is different: the owner usually has to live in the main house or the junior ADU.

Which areas do you serve?

Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Diego and Ventura counties in Southern California.

What documents will I need for financing?

Usually proof of income (W-2s or 1099s, tax returns or transcripts) and recent bank and asset statements. We'll send you a simple checklist for your situation.
