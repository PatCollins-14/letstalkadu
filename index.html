    Quick Answers

    What is an ADU?

    An accessory dwelling unit (ADU) is a second, self-contained home on the same lot as your house, with its own entrance, kitchen, and bathroom. It can be a backyard house, an addition, a converted garage, or a converted room (a junior ADU).

    How much does an ADU cost?

    Most projects cost $60,000 to $450,000. Typical ranges: junior ADU $60,000–$175,000, garage conversion $100,000–$200,000, attached ADU $180,000–$300,000, detached ADU $250,000–$450,000. You get one fixed price in writing, covering design, engineering, permits, and construction, before any work starts.

    Can I finance an ADU?

    Yes. Financing is available, including 0%-interest programs for qualified homeowners, and you see the monthly payment before you commit. If you rent the ADU out, the rent can cover a big part of that payment.

    How long does it take?

    Most projects take about 4–8 months from signed agreement to move-in. Garage conversions are usually fastest. Your city’s permit time is the biggest variable, so we give you an estimate for your city.

    Do you handle the permits?

    Yes. We draw the plans, submit them to the city, and handle any corrections the city asks for at no extra charge. You never have to go to City Hall.

    How big can an ADU be?

    Under California law, a detached ADU can be up to 1,200 square feet. An attached ADU can be up to half the size of your home, up to 1,200 square feet. We check what your lot and city allow during the free consultation.

    Who builds it, and where do you work?

    Design, permits, and construction are done by Mars Construction Industries Corp, a licensed, bonded, and insured builder (California CSLB #1138550) with its own crews. We work in Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Diego, and Ventura counties.
