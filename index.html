    Maximize Your Space With an ADU

    An Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) is a self-contained home on the same lot as your main house, with its own entrance, kitchen, and bathroom. MARS has designed, permitted, and built 100+ of them across Southern California in the last four years. The right type depends on your lot, your budget, and how you plan to use it.

    Detached ADU

    A fully independent home in your backyard.

    Standalone backyard homes, from compact one-bedrooms to full two-bedroom houses. Foundation to finishes and utilities to landscaping tie-in, all in-house.

    Best for:Lots with open backyard space and owners who want the most rental income or a private family suite.

    Attached ADU

    More space, connected to your home.

    An addition built as its own unit, with a new bedroom, bath, kitchen, and entry tied into your home. One team builds the addition and the unit together, so nothing falls between two scopes.

    Best for:Adding living space or rental income without giving up backyard square footage.

    Garage Conversion

    Your fastest path to a rentable unit.

    Usually the fastest path to a second unit. We handle design, structural, plumbing, electrical coordination, HVAC, insulation, and finishes, turning dead garage space into a real home.

    Best for:Homeowners with an unused garage who want the quickest, most budget-friendly ADU.

    Second-Story ADU

    Build up, not out, and keep your yard.

    Second-story units take real structural and engineering work, which is exactly what our A and B license classifications are for.

    Best for:Tight lots, or homeowners who want to keep their garage, parking, and yard.

    ADU + Remodel or New Build

    One contract, one team, no colliding scopes.

    An ADU is often one piece of a bigger project: a remodel, an addition, or a ground-up home. In Altadena, MARS built a new home and ADU from the foundation up.

    Best for:Homeowners planning more than one project who want it all under a single contract.

    Junior ADU (JADU)

    Turn existing space into its own unit.

    Usually a bedroom converted into a compact unit with its own entrance, a kitchenette, and a bath. It stays inside your existing footprint, so it’s often the lowest-cost path to a legal second unit.

    Best for:Rental income or family space without new construction in the yard.

    Common questions

    ADU Questions, Answered

    What is an ADU?

    An accessory dwelling unit (ADU) is a self-contained home on the same lot as your main house, with its own entrance, kitchen, and bathroom. ADUs can be detached backyard homes, attached additions, garage conversions, or second-story units.

    What types of ADUs can you build?

    We build detached ADUs, attached ADUs, garage conversions, second-story ADUs, and junior ADUs (JADUs). An ADU can also be combined with a remodel, an addition, or a ground-up new home under one contract.

    Should I convert my garage or build a detached ADU?

    It depends on your lot, your garage, and what you want the finished home to be. A garage conversion is usually the fastest path to a second unit, while a detached build gives you a true standalone house. We compare both options for your property.

    Do you handle ADU permits?

    Yes. Design, engineering, permit submittals, and city corrections are handled in-house by our licensed builder, so the people answering the city’s questions are the people who drew the plans.

    What is a junior ADU (JADU)?

    A junior ADU is carved out of your existing house, typically a bedroom converted into a compact unit with its own entrance, a kitchenette, and a bathroom. Because it stays inside the existing footprint, it is often the lowest-cost path to a legal second unit.

    Can an ADU be part of a bigger project?

    Yes. An ADU is often one piece of a remodel, an addition, or a ground-up build. One contract and one team keep the scopes from colliding, as on the Altadena project where a new home and ADU were built from the foundation up.

    Which areas do you serve?

    We serve Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Bernardino, San Diego, and Ventura counties in Southern California, with recent work ranging from Irvine and Anaheim to Huntington Beach and Pasadena.

    Is the builder licensed and insured?

    Yes. Mars Construction Industries Corp holds California CSLB license #1138550 with A (General Engineering), B (General Building), C-36 (Plumbing), and C-20 (HVAC) classifications, and is licensed, bonded, and insured.

    Who will I work with?

    You have one point of contact, from your first property walk through move-in day. Mars Construction Industries’ own crews and one supervisor run the design, permits, and construction.
