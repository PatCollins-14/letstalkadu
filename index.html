    Legal & Financial Clarity

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about California ADU mandates, financing, and licensed construction by Mars Construction, CSLB #1138550.

    Do I really not have to replace my garage parking if I convert it?

    Yes. Under California state law (Gov. Code § 66310 et seq.), when a garage, carport, or covered parking structure is converted to an ADU, the local city cannot require replacement off-street parking. Additionally, if your property is within 1/2 mile walking distance of public transit (bus stop, light rail, etc.), the city cannot require any parking for the ADU.

    Why is 749 sq ft such an important cutoff in California?

    Under state statute, any ADU under 750 square feet is 100% exempt from local agency development impact fees. Once you build 750 sq ft or larger, cities can assess impact and school district fees (which can add $4,000 to $9,000+ to your permit costs). Designing to 749 sq ft gives you a full 2-bedroom unit while keeping city impact fees at exactly $0. (School district fees can still apply to ADUs over 500 sq ft.)

    How does in-house ADU lending work without refinancing my low first mortgage?

    If you have a 2.75%–3.5% mortgage from 2020–2021, you should never touch it. We arrange standalone second mortgages, standalone Home Equity Lines of Credit (HELOCs), or renovation loans that sit in 2nd lien position. This leaves your primary ultra-low interest rate intact while funding construction. Depending on the program, Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines can also let ADU rental income help you qualify.

    Will building an ADU trigger a full reassessment of my primary home?

    No. Under California Proposition 13 rules, building an ADU triggers only a blended/supplemental assessment on the new construction value itself (typically ~1.25% of the $100k–$187k build cost). Your primary home's existing low tax assessment base remains protected.
